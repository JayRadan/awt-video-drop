## Growth rules (from the channel's analytics, Oct 2026)
Retention was 27–32% and YouTube's browse/suggested feeds sent almost no traffic; subscribers watched 40%. Breakout gold videos on 1K–40K-sub channels share: viewer-money titles ("If You Hold Gold, Watch This Before…"), a contrarian claim in the first 10 seconds, timing hooks ("before the week ahead"), 5–10 min length, and a question that drives comments. Therefore:
- **Cold open**: the first paragraph of the script is the hook — one contrarian/consequence sentence plus one line on what's at stake, spoken over the live chart (engine scene 0). Set env `HOOK="LINE ONE|LINE TWO"` (upper case, ≤ 45 chars each) from that paragraph. The brand sting plays automatically at the end of scene 0 — no spoken "Anonymous Wolf Trader. Gold update" opener.
- **Title**: written for someone who *holds* gold, not for a chart reader. At most one number. Patterns: "If You Hold Gold, Watch This Before 4,150 Breaks", "Everyone Expects Gold to Break Out. The Options Market Says No", "Gold Week Ahead: Who Is Selling Into 4,150". Score 2–3 candidates with `vidiq_score_title` (channelId, type long) and use the best. Thumbnail text: 2–3 words of consequence ("SELLING INTO IT", "WHO'S SHORT?"), not price labels.
- **Length**: aim for 5–7 minutes (650–1,000 words). Give each signal room: what it is, why it matters, what the chart shows. Do not pad with generic filler; expand only with what the user's analysis supports. If the user's text includes what would change his mind, give it its own paragraph before the conclusion; never invent one.
- **Weekend edition**: if the video is produced Friday–Sunday, title/hook it as "Gold Week Ahead" with the week's dates.
- **Comments**: end the script with a question to the viewer (e.g. "Are you short gold here, or waiting for the break? Tell me in the comments."), set env `CTA` to the same question in upper case for the outro, and post it as the first/pinned comment via Metricool `firstCommentText`.

Merge/split the user's points to fit 10 paragraphs while keeping every claim.
4. **Voice**: `vidiq_voiceover_generate` (script, voiceId Brian) → poll `vidiq_job_poll` → `curl -o voice.mp3 "<audioUrl>"`; keep `durationSeconds`.
5. **Timing**: `ffmpeg -hide_banner -i voice.mp3 -af "silencedetect=noise=-32dB:d=0.3" -f null - 2>&1 | grep -E "silence_(start|end)" | sed -E 's/.*silence_(start|end): ([0-9.]+).*/\1 \2/' > silences.txt` then `python3 timing.py script.txt silences.txt <durationSeconds> 1.0` → `timing.json`.
