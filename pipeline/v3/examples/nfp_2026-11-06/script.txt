When we get a shock like this, with forced liquidation, brokers call the owners of high leverage positions and say: you're getting margin called. Either add capital, or we close your position. In that moment, big funds are bleeding, and they want to keep their positions open. So what do they do? They sell the most liquid asset in their portfolio to cover the margin call. And that is gold. That's why in these situations, gold usually falls even sharper than stocks.

But look what happened. Gold didn't budge. Just 1.5 percent, while the S&P 500 fell 4.4 percent.

What does that tell us? It tells us the positions in stocks really got closed and liquidated. They didn't keep them open. The majority of high leverage positions got washed out yesterday.
